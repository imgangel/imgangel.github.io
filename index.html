Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Private Pilot Written Exam (PAR) and our ground school.

Once you complete our live virtual class or score 80% or higher on three consecutive practice exams in the PAR Exam Prep App, our Chief Flight Instructor will issue your official FAA Knowledge Test Endorsement once your practice results show you are ready (8710 form / digital sign-off). This endorsement is required by PSI testing centers to take your actual written exam.
The official FAA Private Pilot Airplane (PAR) knowledge exam consists of 60 multiple-choice questions. You are given 2.0 hours to complete the exam, and the passing score is 70%. Our app and live class simulate this exact format so you finish with time to spare.
The program includes live interactive evening sessions with active Certified Flight Instructors where you can ask questions in real-time. If you miss a session, HD video recordings are available in your student portal within 2 hours. Black Friday pricing for the ground school is $499 through December 24 (regular price $750).
According to FAA regulations (14 CFR § 61.35), an endorsement for a knowledge test is valid for 24 calendar months from the date it is issued. If you do not take your written exam within 2 years, you simply need a quick review with our CFI team to renew your endorsement.
